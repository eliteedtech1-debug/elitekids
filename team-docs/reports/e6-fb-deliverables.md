## 1. Character Bible — Guardians of the Storm

> **Framing:** These are ancient **Guardians of Knowledge** — wise beings who test children's learning, not villains to be destroyed. When a child defeats a boss, the Guardian **bows and shares a wisdom quote** (lesson takeaway). Wrong answers drain the child's shield; at zero they "retreat and regroup" with encouragement.

## 3. SFX / Art Direction Document

### 3.1 Sound Design Principles

1. **No scary sounds.** No screams, no sword clashes, no death sounds. The boss is a Guardian being outwitted, not destroyed.
2. **Drums are the backbone.** Nigerian talking drums (dundun), djembe, and shakers form the rhythm layer.
3. **Thunder is reverent, not violent.** Low rumble + crackle, not explosive blasts.
4. **Victory = celebration, not destruction.** Choral voices, drumming, laughter — like a festival, not a battlefield.

### 3.2 Sound Effects List

| Trigger | Sound | Source / Style |
|---|---|---|
| Boss entrance | Deep drum roll + low rumble → silence → "I am [Guardian name]" voice-over | Dundun drum roll, 2s fade-in |
| Correct answer | Satisfying "thwack" + chime | Wooden block hit + high bell tone, 0.3s |
| Wrong answer (strike) | Gentle "whoosh" + soft thud | Wind effect + muted drum tap, 0.5s |
| Combo ×2 | Rising drum pattern | Snare roll up, 0.4s |
| Combo ×3 (rage trigger) | Deep thunder crack + drum crescendo | Thunder crack + talking drum burst, 1.5s |

## 5. Teacher Guide — "How to Schedule a Class Raid / Festival of Guardians"

### What Is a Class Raid?

A **Class Raid** is a special boss battle where your **entire class fights together** against one Guardian. Every student's correct answers deal damage to the Guardian's HP bar. The class wins or retreats as a team.

**Think of it like this:** Instead of each child fighting their own boss, the whole class shares ONE giant boss. When Amara answers correctly, the Guardian's health bar moves for everyone. When Chidi answers wrong, the whole class loses a little shield energy.

### How to Set Up a Class Raid

1. **Choose a game** — Go to the game library and pick a game config you want to turn into a raid boss. (Any quiz, matching, or tap-recognition game works.)
2. **Flag as Raid Boss** — In the Staff Panel, toggle "Set as Class Raid" and choose a time window (e.g., "This Friday, 10am–2pm").
3. **Announce to class** — Tell your students: "On Friday, we're fighting the Guardian together! Every game you play counts toward our shared victory."
4. **Watch the progress** — The raid HP bar is visible on every student's Home screen. You can also see it in the Staff Panel with each child's contribution.
5. **Celebrate!** — When the Guardian is defeated, the whole class sees a victory celebration. The Guardian shares a wisdom quote.

### Tips for Class Raids

- **Pick a game most of your students can do.** The raid works best when everyone can contribute at least a few correct answers.
- **Set a 2–4 hour window** — long enough for all kids to play, short enough to keep excitement high.
- **Use it as review** — Raid bosses are perfect for end-of-week review before a test.
- **Celebrate the team, not just top contributors.** "WE did this together!"

### What Is the Festival of Guardians?

The **Festival of Guardians** is a **term-end mega event** — the biggest celebration of the term. It combines:

- **A series-wide Class Raid** — the whole school (or each class) fights a special Guardian boss.
- **An individual leaderboard** — each child's total score across the Festival determines their rank.
- **Guardian Badges** — the top 3 students in each class earn exclusive badges:
  - 🌟 **Voice of Ṣàngó** — for the overall champion
  - 🌟 **Anansi's Riddle-Master** — for the runner-up
  - 🌟 **Amina's Shield-Bearer** — for third place

### How to Schedule a Festival

1. **Go to Staff Panel → Festival of Guardians.**
2. **Choose the dates** — typically the last academic week of the term.
3. **Select which game series to include** — you can run a Festival across one or more series (e.g., "All Animals games this term").
4. **Preview the badges** — see what badges will be awarded.
5. **Publish** — students will see the Festival countdown on their Home screen.
6. **During the Festival** — students play any game in the selected series. Their scores accumulate on the Festival leaderboard.
7. **At the end** — podium is computed, badges are minted, and a class-wide celebration screen appears.

### Tips for Festivals

- **Announce it a week early** — build excitement! "The Festival of Guardians is coming next week!"
- **Review beforehand** — the Festival rewards students who've practiced all term, so encourage review in the days leading up.
- **Make it a class event** — if you have a projector, show the leaderboard during the last day.
- **Celebrate everyone** — even students who don't make the podium have contributed. "Every one of you helped defeat the Guardian!"

### Key Differences: Class Raid vs Festival

| Feature | Class Raid 🛡️ | Festival of Guardians 🌟 |
|---|---|---|
| **Duration** | A few hours (single session) | Full academic week |
| **Who fights** | Class together (shared HP) | Individual (personal score) |
| **Awards** | Class-wide victory celebration | 🥇🥈🥉 Guardian badges for top 3 |
| **Best for** | Review sessions, team bonding | Term-end celebration, merit awards |
| **Boss type** | Single Guardian from a game | Series-wide (multiple Guardians) |
| **Setup** | Flag a game as raid_boss | Schedule via Festival panel |
