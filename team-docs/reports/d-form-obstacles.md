## Non-obstacle notes
- Login/JWT, requireStaff gating, series CRUD, unit prereq validation (lower unit_number enforced), idempotent progress endpoints: all behaved exactly as documented.
- Approve flow DID correctly run asset-saver and flip lesson+config+scene rows once approval rows existed ("Approved 2 item(s)." for quiz+scenes lesson).
- The manual endpoint's optional `scenes` array works end-to-end when authored as wrapper objects (verified player-visible on d-ani-quiz-kg2).
- SVG data-URI art passes CachedImg passthrough (`data:` prefix short-circuit) — viable offline-safe asset strategy worth promoting into the MediaLibrary.
