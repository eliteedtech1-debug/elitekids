# Nursery 2 Lesson Notes Standard

## Literacy note

Build emergent reading through oral language, shared books, environmental print, rhyme, sound play and
meaning. A child should understand what is read, not merely repeat sounds. Use picture cues, names,
local words and familiar experiences. Allow children to answer orally, point, draw or dictate to an adult.

## Numeracy note

Represent numbers with objects, fingers, movement, marks and numerals. Children should see that the
numeral 7 represents seven objects. Compare and explain; do not rely on reciting a sequence.

## Pattern and inquiry note

Ask children to notice, predict and explain. “How do you know?” can be answered by showing, moving,
pointing or speaking. Use materials from the environment and return them safely.

## Assessment evidence

Collect brief notes, dated drawings, photographs only with consent, dictated sentences, practical
counts and child explanations. Use `independent / prompted / emerging / not yet observed`.

## Digital adaptation

Use 3–5 options, simple sequence/matching/drag-sort games and read all text aloud. Offer a concrete
warm-up first. For emergent reading, image-to-word and audio-supported recognition comes before typed
recall. Keep one topic per game and provide a calm retry.
