# Nursery 2 Scheme of Work (4–5)

Each term has **10 teaching weeks**. Every subject/domain receives at least **one game per week**, with practical exploration before digital practice.

| Week | Communication / reading | Numeracy / patterns | Science / social / health | Creative / physical |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | oral story retell and class language | count/represent 1–5 | classroom agreements | movement review |
| 2 | blend oral sounds in names | numerals 1–5 | family and community | family role play |
| 3 | CVC picture words orally | count 1–10 with objects | body and senses | body rhythm |
| 4 | match word to picture | more/less/equal in small groups | healthy food and water | fruit printing |
| 5 | initial/final sound listening | AB and ABB patterns | plants and growth | garden observation |
| 6 | read familiar labels | shapes and positional words | animals and homes | animal movement |
| 7 | shared reading: beginning/middle/end | number order 1–10 | weather and clothing | weather chart/art |
| 8 | build simple words with cards | compose groups to 5 | materials: hard/soft, float/sink | investigation stations |
| 9 | sentence oral rehearsal | compare length/height | transport and road safety | obstacle course |
| 10 | retell and answer who/where/what | missing item in a pattern | community helpers | dramatic play |
| 10 | story celebration and portfolio | practical review 1–10 | independence conference | child-choice showcase |

**Second Term:** expand familiar word sets and contexts through community, garden and home experiences.
**Third Term:** transfer reading readiness, number, pattern and independence skills into school-readiness contexts. Introduce simple recording through drawings, marks, labels and teacher scribing. Each subject row maps to one Tier 1–2 game seed per week.
