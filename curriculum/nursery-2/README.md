# Nursery 2 (4–5 years)

**Platform band:** `KG1` · **Primary purpose:** numbers to 10, emergent reading, patterns, classification,
oral reasoning, independence and school readiness.

Teach through manipulatives, stories, songs, outdoor investigation and short guided tasks. Children
should explain and demonstrate before being asked to complete paper or digital work independently.

See `subjects/subject-map.md`, `schemes/three-term-scheme.md`, `lesson-plans/sample-weekly-plans.md`
and `lesson-notes/standard-notes.md`.
