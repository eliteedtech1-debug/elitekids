# Nursery 2 Sample Lesson Plans

## Plan 1 — Number Garden 1–10

- **Subject:** Numeracy · **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Objective:** Child counts a set to 10 and matches it to a numeral.
- **Materials:** ten large leaves/flowers or counters, numeral cards 1–10, ten-frame mat.
- **Sequence:** count real objects; touch and move each; build 1–5 then 6–10; match cards; ask “What do you notice?”; let pairs create a garden.
- **Assessment:** accurate one-to-one count, quantity-to-symbol match or explanation with support.
- **Differentiation:** work within 1–5; extend by comparing two groups or ordering cards.

## Plan 2 — Build a Word

- **Subject:** Communication and Reading · **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Objective:** Child blends sounds orally and builds a familiar word such as `cat`, `sun` or `map` using cards.
- **Materials:** picture, three sound/letter cards, magnetic letters.
- **Sequence:** say word naturally; stretch sounds; children push cards together; read whole word; match to picture; use word in an oral sentence.
- **Assessment:** joins oral blend, orders cards, recognises whole word or explains picture.
- **Guardrail:** use familiar words and never shame a child for not blending yet.

## Plan 3 — What Comes Next?

- **Subject:** Patterns · **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Objective:** Child extends an AB or ABB pattern and describes the repeating unit.
- **Materials:** coloured blocks, bottle caps or natural objects.
- **Sequence:** make red-blue; clap the pattern; cover final item; ask what comes next; try ABB; children create a movement pattern.
- **Assessment:** predicts or extends with/without prompt.

## Plan 4 — Float or Sink?

- **Subject:** Pre-science · **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Objective:** Child makes a simple prediction and observes whether safe objects float or sink.
- **Materials:** basin, water, large safe objects (leaf, plastic cup, stone, sponge).
- **Sequence:** show object; ask “What do you think?”; test one at a time; sort picture cards; discuss what happened.
- **Safety:** adult supervises water; dry floor immediately; no small objects.
