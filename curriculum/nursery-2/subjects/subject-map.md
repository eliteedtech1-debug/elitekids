# Nursery 2 Subjects and Domains

| Subject/domain | Scope for ages 4–5 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Communication, Reading and Phonics | letter-sound links, blending orally, high-frequency/familiar words, story comprehension | reads/constructs familiar word or retells meaning |
| Writing Skill | name, controlled lines/curves, selected letters and labels | makes purposeful marks with increasing control |
| Numeracy / Mathematics | count, read and represent 1–10; compare; compose/decompose small groups | matches numeral/quantity and explains more/less |
| Patterns and Shapes | extend AB/ABB patterns; identify 2D shapes; positional words | predicts next item and describes features |
| Pre-science and Nature | living/nonliving, plants, animals, materials, weather, simple prediction | sorts, observes and records drawing/oral statement |
| Social Habits and Citizenship | family/community, cooperation, respect, safety and problem solving | negotiates turn and follows shared rule |
| Health Habits | nutrition, hygiene, exercise, rest, body safety and trusted adults | explains/acts routine |
| Physical Development | balance, throwing/catching, dance, cutting, threading and construction | coordinates movement and tool use |
| Creative Arts, Music and Culture | rhythm, songs, drawing, painting, drama, local stories and crafts | creates, performs and talks about work |
| Digital Readiness | matching, sequence, image-to-word games with voice support | uses simple interaction safely |
