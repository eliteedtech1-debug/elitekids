# Nursery 1 Subjects and Domains

| Subject/domain | Scope for ages 3–4 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Communication and Phonics | listening, rhyme, initial sounds, selected letter shapes/names, picture vocabulary | identifies sound/picture or joins rhyme |
| Pre-writing / Writing Skill | lines, curves, circles, name marks, correct/comfortable grip | makes controlled marks; no handwriting pressure |
| Numeracy / Mathematics | count and match 1–5, one-to-one correspondence, compare, sort | counts objects with touch and matches numeral where ready |
| Shapes, Colours and Patterns | primary colours, circle/square/triangle, AB patterns | extends or copies simple pattern |
| Pre-science and Nature | body, senses, animals, plants, weather, materials | observes, predicts simply, describes |
| Social Habits | family, friends, manners, feelings, sharing and classroom rules | follows routine and communicates need |
| Health Habits | handwashing, food, teeth, exercise, rest, personal safety | demonstrates routine with less prompting |
| Physical Development | locomotor movement, balance, fine-motor manipulation | navigates safely and uses tools |
| Creative Arts, Music and Rhymes | singing, beat, drawing, painting, collage, role play | creates/participates and explains choice |
| Digital Readiness | optional matching/tap game with audio and 2–4 choices | recognition without reading demand |
