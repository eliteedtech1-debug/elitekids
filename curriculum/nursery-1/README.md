# Nursery 1 (3–4 years)

**Platform band:** `Nursery` · **Primary purpose:** oral language, early phonological awareness, ABC
exposure, counting 1–5, matching, self-care and cooperative play.

Use short, concrete, multisensory sessions. Children may be at very different stages; recognise
participation and approximation. Formal reading and writing are emerging, not prerequisites.

See `subjects/subject-map.md`, `schemes/three-term-scheme.md`, `lesson-plans/sample-weekly-plans.md`
and `lesson-notes/standard-notes.md`.
