# Nursery 1 Sample Lesson Plans

## Plan 1 — Count and Match 1–5

- **Subject:** Numeracy · **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Objective:** Child counts a group of up to five objects by touching each once and matches it to a numeral when ready.
- **Materials:** bottle tops/large counters, numeral cards 1–5, five hoops or mats.
- **Sequence:** count slowly together; model touch-and-move; build groups 1, 2 and 3; invite pairs; extend to 5 only when secure; sing a counting song.
- **Assessment:** observe one-to-one counting and matching; accept pointing, oral counting or adult-supported counting.
- **Differentiation:** use 1–3 objects, larger counters or a ten-frame-style mat; extend by asking “How many altogether?” only for ready children.
- **Home link:** count spoons, fruits or steps.

## Plan 2 — Find the First Sound

- **Subject:** Communication and Phonics · **Duration:** 12 minutes
- **Objective:** Child notices that familiar words can begin with the same sound.
- **Materials:** picture cards/objects for sun, sock, bag, ball; letter cards optional.
- **Sequence:** say “sun—sock” with emphasis; children listen and repeat if comfortable; sort two picture groups by sound; introduce A/B only as visual labels after oral play.
- **Assessment:** points to a same-sound pair, imitates sound or explains with home-language support.
- **Guardrail:** do not teach letter-name memorisation as the only outcome.

## Plan 3 — AB Colour Pattern Train

- **Subject:** Patterns / Creative Arts · **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Objective:** Child copies or extends a red-blue repeating pattern.
- **Materials:** large red/blue paper shapes, blocks or bottle caps.
- **Sequence:** make red-blue; say the pattern; let children continue; build a “train”; invite children to invent a two-item pattern.
- **Assessment:** continues pattern with or without prompt.
- **Inclusion:** use tactile shapes, movement pattern (clap/stamp) or fewer items.

## Plan 4 — Healthy Hands and Teeth

- **Subject:** Health Habits · **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Objective:** Child demonstrates key steps in handwashing and names/acts one tooth-care habit.
- **Sequence:** puppet story; sequence picture cards; practise wash/rinse/dry; demonstrate brush movement with a large model; discuss water and healthy foods.
- **Assessment:** sequences two or more steps and participates safely.
