# Nursery 1 Scheme of Work (3–4)

Each term has **10 teaching weeks**. Every subject/domain receives at least **one game per week**, preceded by concrete play and followed by observation.

| Week | Communication / literacy | Numeracy / shapes / patterns | Science / social / health | Creative / movement |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | names, listening rules, rhyme | review colours and same/different | classroom routines and safe body | welcome songs and movement |
| 2 | rhyme: same ending sounds | count 1–2 real objects | family members and care | family drawing |
| 3 | letter exposure A/B through sound and story | count 1–3; match groups | body parts and senses | body-action song |
| 4 | letter C; picture vocabulary | circle and square | handwashing sequence | shape printing |
| 5 | listen for first sound in familiar words | count 1–5; one-to-one | domestic animals and care | animal sound/drama |
| 6 | letter D/E; oral story sequence | compare big/small, more/fewer | healthy food and water | fruit collage |
| 7 | review A–E through rhyme | AB colour pattern | weather and clothing | weather dance |
| 8 | letter F/G; name marks | triangle; sort by colour | plants and what they need | leaf rubbing |
| 9 | picture-to-word matching | number shapes 1–5 | community helpers | role play |
| 10 | oral retell with 3 pictures | match numeral to quantity 1–5 | road/home safety | movement route |
| 10 | term review through story | practical count/match review | observation and celebration | portfolio sharing |

**Second Term:** revisit A–G and introduce a small number of new letters in meaningful contexts.
**Third Term:** transfer sounds, counting and routines into home/community contexts. Keep the main goal oral language and sound awareness rather than alphabet memorisation. Each subject row maps to one Tier 1 game seed per week.
