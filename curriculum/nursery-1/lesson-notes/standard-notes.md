# Nursery 1 Lesson Notes Standard

## Language and interaction

Use short sentences, clear modelling, songs, picture cues and wait time. Introduce English alongside
children's familiar language rather than treating home language as a problem. Recast errors kindly:
child: “tar”; adult: “Yes, car. The car is red.”

## Early literacy

Prioritise listening, rhyme, syllables, sound play, picture vocabulary, story enjoyment and mark-making.
Letter cards should support a meaningful word or story. Avoid copying pages of letters or requiring a
child to read words before oral language is ready.

## Numeracy

Always start with real objects and actions. Children touch/move one item per count word. Ask “How many
did we make?” rather than rewarding fast recitation. Use numbers 1–5 as a practical range; individual
children may need 1–3 or may be ready to explore beyond it.

## Assessment record

For each objective mark: `observed independently`, `observed with prompt`, `emerging`, or `not yet
observed`. Add the context and next activity. Avoid percentages or public comparison.

## Digital adaptation

Use matching, tap-recognition and simple drag-sort only after physical practice. Provide 3–4 large
choices, read prompts aloud, allow audio replay and avoid timer pressure. Recognition comes before recall;
keep one objective per game.
