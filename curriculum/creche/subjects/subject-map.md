# Crèche Subjects and Domains

| Subject/domain | Scope for ages 0–2 | Suggested evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Sensory and Discovery | touch, texture, colour, light, sound, object permanence | reaches, explores, repeats an action |
| Communication and Early Literacy | name response, songs, gestures, babble, picture sharing | looks, points, imitates, vocalises |
| Numeracy Foundations | action songs, big/small, more/less, one object at a time | joins counting routine; explores quantity |
| Pre-science and Nature | water, leaves, safe natural objects, pets and weather | notices, touches, watches, shows curiosity |
| Social-Emotional / Social Habits | attachment, turn-taking, trusted adults, simple choices | seeks comfort; accepts brief turn |
| Health and Self-care | handwashing, clean-up, feeding, rest and safe boundaries | participates with support |
| Physical Development | tummy time, crawling, walking, reaching, grasping and dancing | movement progress and coordination |
| Creative Arts, Music and Rhymes | lullabies, beat, clapping, scarves, finger painting | responds to rhythm/colour; makes marks |
| Digital Readiness | optional adult-led sound/colour reveal, max 3–5 minutes | attention and enjoyment, not scores |

**Not expected:** alphabet drills, worksheets, forced speech, independent device use or formal assessment.
