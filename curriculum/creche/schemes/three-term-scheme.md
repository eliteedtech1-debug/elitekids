# Crèche Scheme of Work (0–2)

Use one main experience daily, repeat it through songs and routines, and adjust the duration to the child. Each term has **10 teaching weeks** and every listed domain receives at least **one adult-led game per week**. The table is a planning spine, not a rigid timetable.

| Week | Sensory/discovery | Communication/rhymes | Numeracy/concepts | Social, health and movement |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | settling: familiar faces and safe objects | name song | one/more through snack | comfort, handwashing, floor movement |
| 2 | soft/rough and smooth | babble and imitation | big/small | reaching, grasping, turn-taking |
| 3 | red/yellow/blue objects | animal sounds | one object in/out | crawling/walking; clean-up |
| 4 | water play (close supervision) | action song: splash/stop | full/empty | pouring with adult help; waiting |
| 5 | light and shadow | picture talk | in/out, up/down | dancing and pause signal |
| 6 | sound baskets: shaker/drum | loud/quiet | one beat/two beats | clapping; gentle instrument use |
| 7 | leaves and safe nature walk | weather words | more/fewer leaves | outdoor boundaries; squat/reach |
| 8 | colour hunt | repeat a refrain | same/different | carrying and placing objects |
| 9 | texture collage | body-part song | near/far | pointing, crawling route |
| 10 | calm review and celebration | child-choice songs | revisit concepts naturally | observation conference with caregiver |

**Second Term:** repeat the 10-week progression with family, market, water, rain and garden contexts.
**Third Term:** repeat the 10-week progression with food, transport, community helpers and familiar home routines. Do not increase abstraction simply because a term has changed; increase familiarity, choice and participation first. Each domain row maps to one Tier 0 game seed per week.
