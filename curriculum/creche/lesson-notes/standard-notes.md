# Crèche Lesson Notes Standard

## Delivery principles

- Begin with connection: name the child, make eye contact if welcomed, smile and wait.
- Follow the child’s lead. A child may observe before touching; observation is participation.
- Use one short phrase with a gesture: “red cup”, “in”, “wash hands”, “my turn/your turn”.
- Repeat predictable songs and routines. Repetition builds security and learning.
- Offer two choices rather than a demand. Stop when the child turns away, becomes distressed or shows fatigue.
- Use real objects and body movement before any screen. A digital activity is optional and adult-led.

## Observation record

Record date, context, what happened, level of support (`independent`, `with prompt`, `not yet`), and
next opportunity. Example: “12 Sept — sensory basket — reached for yellow cloth and transferred it to
basket independently; smiled when song repeated; offer two-colour choice next time.”

## Teacher language

Say: “You found it.” “I hear your sound.” “You are safe.” “More or finished?” “Let us try together.”
Avoid: “Say it properly.” “You are naughty.” “Everybody else can do it.”

## Inclusion and wellbeing

Provide quiet space, adaptive grip, alternate textures, extra processing time and home-language words.
Respect feeding, sleep, toileting, sensory and medical plans. Never use food as a reward or withhold
comfort. Obtain consent before photographs or recordings.

## Digital adaptation

If this note becomes an EliteKids activity, use Tier 0 exposure only: one large image or colour, one
friendly sound, no timer, no incorrect-answer sound, and a caregiver beside the child. Stop after a
few minutes and record the real-world observation rather than a score.
