# Crèche (0–2 years)

**Platform band:** `Creche` · **Primary purpose:** care, attachment, sensory exploration and early communication.

This pack is for babies and toddlers, not formal academic instruction. Follow each child's developmental pace, health plan and family language. Adults stay close during every activity. Use large, washable, non-toxic materials with no small detachable parts.

## Core outcomes

- feels safe, connected and ready to explore;
- responds to familiar voices, songs and simple sounds;
- explores colour, texture, light, movement and cause/effect;
- communicates needs through gesture, sound, eye gaze and emerging words;
- develops crawling, walking, balance, grasp and release;
- begins to participate in feeding, handwashing and tidy-up routines.
