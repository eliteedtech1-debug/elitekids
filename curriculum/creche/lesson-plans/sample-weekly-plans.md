# Crèche Sample Lesson Plans

## Plan 1 — Red, Yellow and Blue Discovery

- **Class/subject:** Crèche · Sensory and Discovery
- **Duration:** 5–8 minutes, repeated in small groups
- **Objective:** Child explores and shows interest in coloured objects.
- **Materials:** large fabric squares or cups in three colours; basket; familiar song.
- **Sequence:** greet child; name one colour slowly; offer two safe objects; allow touching and mouthing only if materials are safe; sing a colour song; let the child choose; model “in” and “out”.
- **Assessment:** looks, reaches, chooses, transfers or vocalises. Do not require colour naming.
- **Differentiation:** use one colour and high-contrast objects for a child needing less stimulation; offer seated or floor position.
- **Home link:** caregiver notices one colour during dressing or mealtime.
- **Safety:** no small objects, cords, sharp edges or unsupervised water.

## Plan 2 — Farm Sound and Movement

- **Class/subject:** Crèche · Communication / Music
- **Duration:** 5 minutes
- **Objective:** Child responds to a familiar animal sound and copies a movement or sound if ready.
- **Materials:** large animal pictures or soft toys; “moo”, “baa”, “woof” voice sounds.
- **Sequence:** show one picture; say the sound; pause for response; rock or move like the animal; invite imitation; finish with “bye-bye animal”.
- **Assessment:** gaze, smile, body movement, gesture, babble or sound imitation.
- **Differentiation:** use the child’s home-language animal name; accept looking only.
- **Home link:** identify a familiar animal at home or in the community.

## Plan 3 — One Cup for One Friend

- **Class/subject:** Crèche · Numeracy Foundations / Social Habits
- **Duration:** 5–7 minutes
- **Objective:** Child participates in giving one object to one adult or peer.
- **Materials:** large cups or soft blocks; two adults/children; tray.
- **Sequence:** model “one for you”; give one cup; clap gently; repeat with another child; say “more” only if the child reaches for another.
- **Assessment:** releases object, watches exchange, accepts turn or requests more.
- **Differentiation:** hand-over-hand support only with consent and gentle touch.

## Plan 4 — Wash, Wipe, Wave

- **Class/subject:** Crèche · Health and Self-care
- **Duration:** routine-based, 3–5 minutes
- **Objective:** Child joins a hand-cleaning routine with adult support.
- **Sequence:** show water; name “wash”; rub hands; rinse; dry; wave clean hands. Pair each step with the same words and song.
- **Assessment:** reaches for water, rubs hands, tolerates drying or anticipates the next step.
- **Safety:** adult controls water temperature and supervises continuously.
