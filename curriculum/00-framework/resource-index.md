## Suggested next expansion

The current packs are planning foundations: each has one subject map, one three-term scheme, four
sample lesson plans and lesson-note guidance. Before calling them a complete school scheme, expand
each subject into separate weekly notes and add:

- termly learning outcomes and observation rubrics;
- local state-specific objectives and approved textbook references;
- home-language vocabulary lists (for example Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo or the school's community language);
- inclusion adaptations for identified learners;
- printable picture/observation cards;
- teacher review/approval metadata and curriculum codes once the school confirms the official source;
- digital-game configs only after the lesson has been reviewed by an ECCE specialist.
