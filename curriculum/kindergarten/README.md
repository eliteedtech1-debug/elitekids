# Kindergarten (5–6 years)

**Platform band:** `KG2` · **Primary purpose:** simple addition, familiar words, short sentences,
patterns, reasoning, independence and transition to primary school.

Keep learning concrete, playful and language-rich. Use counters and stories before equations, pictures
and oral rehearsal before sentence writing, and practical demonstration before worksheets.

See `subjects/subject-map.md`, `schemes/three-term-scheme.md`, `lesson-plans/sample-weekly-plans.md`
and `lesson-notes/standard-notes.md`.
