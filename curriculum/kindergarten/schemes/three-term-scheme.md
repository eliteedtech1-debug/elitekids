# Kindergarten Scheme of Work (5–6)

Each term has **10 teaching weeks**. Every subject/domain receives at least **one game per week**, after concrete modelling and oral rehearsal.

| Week | English / writing | Mathematics / patterns | Science / social / health | Creative / school readiness |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | oral sentences about self | count/review 1–10 | classroom jobs and safety | cooperative games |
| 2 | familiar names and labels | join groups of 1 and 2 | family/community | family drawing |
| 3 | CVC word building | addition stories within 5 | food, water and hygiene | market role play |
| 4 | read a short patterned sentence | addition with counters within 5 | body, senses and exercise | body rhythm |
| 5 | sentence punctuation orally | number bonds to 5 | plants and needs | garden art |
| 6 | picture sequence retell | patterns AB/ABB/ABC | animals and care | drama and movement |
| 7 | high-frequency/familiar words | numbers 11–20 through objects | weather and clothing | weather chart |
| 8 | write/dictate 2–4 word sentences | compare length/capacity | materials and simple tests | construction challenge |
| 9 | question/answer from a story | add within 5 in a context | transport and road safety | obstacle course |
| 10 | compose a sentence from a picture | missing item in patterns | community helpers | pretend community |
| 10 | portfolio story and sentence | practical number review | school-readiness reflection | celebration/showcase |

**Second Term:** extend addition toward 10 only when children model and explain within 5 securely.
**Third Term:** transfer number, reading, writing and reasoning into primary-school readiness contexts. Increase sentence length gradually and preserve oral rehearsal, drawing and teacher scribing as valid routes to expression. Each subject row maps to one Tier 1–3 game seed per week.
