# Kindergarten Lesson Notes Standard

## Mathematics

Use objects, fingers, drawings and stories before symbols. For simple addition, the key concept is
joining groups, not memorising facts. Ask “Show me how you know.” Begin within 5 and extend toward 10
only when the child can model and explain totals.

## Reading and writing

Use shared reading, phonics, familiar words, oral sentence rehearsal, environmental print and drawing.
Accept invented spelling and dictation as developmentally useful. Correct by modelling, not by erasing
or shaming. Meaning and communication come first.

## School readiness and social learning

Practise following routines, carrying belongings, asking for help, taking turns, listening in a group,
solving a small problem and managing transitions. Readiness is social, emotional, physical and
cognitive—not only handwriting and arithmetic.

## Assessment record

Use dated work samples and short observations. Record whether the child demonstrated the objective
independently, with a prompt, or is emerging. Include the child's explanation. Keep assessment low-stakes
and use it to plan the next experience.

## Digital adaptation

Quiz, fill-in-the-blank, matching and drag-sort may be used for reinforcement after concrete play.
Read prompts aloud, use 3–5 choices, avoid punitive sounds and timers in Learning mode, and keep one
objective per game. A Test mode should not be offered until the child's learning/practice sequence is
complete according to EliteKids rules.
