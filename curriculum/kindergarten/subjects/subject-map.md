# Kindergarten Subjects and Domains

| Subject/domain | Scope for ages 5–6 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| English / Early Reading | familiar word reading, phonics review, oral comprehension, short sentence construction | reads/builds familiar words; retells and composes sentence |
| Writing Skill | name, letters, words and short meaningful sentences with developing control | labels/draws/writes for a purpose |
| Mathematics | numbers to 20 as appropriate; joining groups; simple addition within 5, then 10 when ready | models and explains addition with objects/pictures |
| Patterns, Shapes and Measurement | repeating patterns, 2D/3D shapes, length, capacity, position and comparison | extends pattern and explains property |
| Basic Science and Nature | living things, materials, weather, health, observation and simple cause/effect | predicts, tests, draws or reports |
| Social Habits / Citizenship | identity, family, community, fairness, cooperation, safety and responsibility | participates, solves simple social problem |
| Health and Physical Education | hygiene, nutrition, exercise, rest, body safety and movement skills | demonstrates safe routine and coordination |
| Creative Arts, Music and Culture | visual art, rhythm, songs, drama, dance, local stories and crafts | creates, performs and reflects |
| Digital Readiness | short supervised game/story aligned to one objective | navigates, listens and stops safely |
