# Kindergarten Sample Lesson Plans

## Plan 1 — Add the Mangoes

- **Subject:** Mathematics · **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Objective:** Child joins two groups and represents the total within 5.
- **Materials:** five mango/fruit counters or pictures, two hoops, numeral cards 1–5.
- **Sequence:** tell a story: “Kemi has 2 mangoes. Mama gives 1 more.” Build group 2, add group 1, move together and count; show 2 + 1 = 3; children make their own story.
- **Assessment:** models joining, counts total or chooses numeral; explanation may be oral, gesture or drawing.
- **Differentiation:** use 1+1 with two objects; extend to totals 10 only for ready children.
- **Guardrail:** no timed arithmetic or public ranking.

## Plan 2 — Build and Read a Sentence

- **Subject:** English / Writing · **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Objective:** Child builds or dictates a short sentence from a familiar picture.
- **Materials:** picture of a child watering a plant; word cards `I`, `see`, `a`, `plant`, punctuation card.
- **Sequence:** discuss picture; model oral sentence; children arrange cards; read together; draw and label; invite independent dictation/writing.
- **Assessment:** communicates meaning, orders some words or attempts writing.
- **Differentiation:** use two-word sentence or symbol/picture cards; extend with one describing word.

## Plan 3 — Pattern Detectives

- **Subject:** Patterns / Reasoning · **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Objective:** Child extends an AB, ABB or ABC pattern and explains what repeats.
- **Materials:** blocks, bottle caps, beads too large to swallow, movement cards.
- **Sequence:** make pattern; children clap it; hide last item; predict; build a class pattern; photograph/work-sample only with consent.
- **Assessment:** extends or corrects pattern and communicates reason.

## Plan 4 — School Day Safety Story

- **Subject:** Social Habits / Health · **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Objective:** Child identifies one safe response in a school routine and names a trusted adult.
- **Sequence:** puppet story about arrival, handwashing, crossing a doorway and asking for help; children sequence picture cards; role-play “stop, stay, tell”.
- **Assessment:** chooses safe action, identifies trusted adult or rehearses help phrase.
- **Safeguarding:** do not ask children to disclose personal incidents in the group; follow school safeguarding procedures.
