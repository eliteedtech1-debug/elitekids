# Playgroup Scheme of Work (2–3)

Each term has **10 teaching weeks**. Every subject/domain receives at least **one supervised game per week**; the game remains short, concrete and observation-led.

| Week | Communication/literacy | Numeracy/shapes/colours | Nature/creative | Social, health and movement |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | welcome names and classroom words | match same objects | favourite song and free art | settling and safe walking |
| 2 | family picture talk | red/yellow; big/small | family collage | greeting and handwashing |
| 3 | animal sounds | count 1–2 actions | animal movement song | gentle touch and turn-taking |
| 4 | rhyme with actions | circle hunt | circle printing | stop/go movement |
| 5 | listen and find a picture | count 1–3 blocks | block building | tidy-up routine |
| 6 | body-part song | square hunt; same/different | body outline art | personal boundaries |
| 7 | weather words | more/fewer fruit pictures | rain sound/rhythm | sun/rain safety |
| 8 | retell a 2-picture story | triangle hunt | triangle collage | sharing materials |
| 9 | transport sounds | long/short; near/far | vehicle painting | road and car safety |
| 10 | repeat a story refrain | match animal pairs | pretend farm | caring for animals || 10 | story/song celebration | practical review | portfolio/play gallery | family feedback and next steps |

**Second Term:** spiral the same skills through market, garden, water, community helper and cultural contexts.
**Third Term:** repeat and transfer through home, transport, food and cultural contexts. Introduce only a few new words or objects at a time. Each subject row maps to one Tier 0–1 game seed per week.
