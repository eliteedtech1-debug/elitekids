# Playgroup Subjects and Domains

| Subject/domain | Age-appropriate scope | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Colours and Sensory Discovery | identify/point to common colours; texture, light, water and sand play | sorts or chooses by colour with support |
| Shapes and Construction | circle, square, triangle through blocks, puzzles and movement | matches identical shapes; builds |
| Communication and Early Literacy | picture naming, rhyme, listening, story sequence and home-language support | points, answers, joins refrain |
| Numeracy Foundations | 1–3 through songs and objects; same/different; big/small; more/fewer | gives or counts a small group |
| Animals and Pre-science | familiar domestic/wild animals, sounds, body parts, weather and plants | matches animal to picture/sound |
| Social Habits | greetings, turn-taking, feelings, family and classroom routines | waits briefly, chooses, seeks help |
| Health Habits | handwashing, clean food/water, toileting and safe boundaries | follows routine with prompts |
| Movement and Physical Play | walk, run, jump, balance, throw/roll, finger actions | joins movement safely |
| Creative Arts, Music and Rhymes | singing, rhythm, pretend play, painting, collage and dance | explores and creates |
| Digital Readiness | optional adult-led colour/shape/animal recognition | taps a large target; no score pressure |
