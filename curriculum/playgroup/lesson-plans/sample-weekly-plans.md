# Playgroup Sample Lesson Plans

## Plan 1 — Shape Safari

- **Subject:** Shapes and Construction · **Duration:** 10 minutes
- **Objective:** Child matches a circle, square or triangle to the same shape.
- **Materials:** large cardboard shapes, hoops, blocks, classroom objects.
- **Sequence:** sing “look and find”; show one circle and trace it; find a round object; let children carry/match shapes; finish with body-making a circle.
- **Assessment:** points, matches or names a shape; accept pointing and home-language naming.
- **Differentiation:** begin with one shape; use high-contrast outlines and hand-over-hand modelling only when welcomed.
- **Safety:** use large, blunt-edged shapes; keep floor clear.

## Plan 2 — Three Little Animals

- **Subject:** Animals / Numeracy · **Duration:** 8–10 minutes
- **Objective:** Child joins one-to-one counting of up to three animal figures and matches a sound.
- **Materials:** three large animal toys/pictures, basket, sound cards.
- **Sequence:** introduce cow, dog and hen; make one sound at a time; place one animal in basket while counting; invite children to give one animal to each picture mat.
- **Assessment:** gives, matches, points, imitates or joins counting.
- **Home link:** count three safe household objects together.

## Plan 3 — Feelings Faces

- **Subject:** Social Habits / Communication · **Duration:** 8 minutes
- **Objective:** Child recognises or communicates happy, sad and tired using a face, gesture or word.
- **Sequence:** show three friendly faces; model each expression; sing a calm song; children choose a face; practise “help please” and “finished”.
- **Assessment:** chooses or imitates; never demand disclosure of a private feeling.
- **Inclusion:** provide emotion cards, AAC/gesture choices and quiet observation.

## Plan 4 — Wash the Teddy’s Hands

- **Subject:** Health Habits / Pretend Play · **Duration:** 10 minutes
- **Objective:** Child sequences wet–soap–rub–rinse–dry with a toy and adult model.
- **Materials:** teddy, basin or pretend kit, towel, soap under adult control.
- **Assessment:** joins one or more steps and anticipates routine.
