# Playgroup Lesson Notes Standard

## Teaching notes

- Keep group activities short and alternate sitting with movement.
- Use visual demonstration before verbal explanation.
- Give two choices and wait at least five seconds for processing.
- Accept pointing, gesture, home language, emerging English, sound imitation and drawing as valid communication.
- Repeat key words naturally instead of drilling them.
- Keep stories to a few pictures and let children act them out.

## Observation prompts

Can the child match identical objects? Point to a requested colour/shape? Follow one or two routine
steps? Join a rhyme? Give one object to a person? Ask for help or indicate “finished”? Record context
and prompts; do not score speed.

## Suggested vocabulary

`same`, `different`, `more`, `one`, `two`, `three`, `big`, `small`, `in`, `out`, `circle`, `red`, `dog`,
`cat`, `wash`, `help`, `stop`, `go`, `happy`, `sad`, `tired`.

## Digital adaptation

Use matching or tap-recognition only after real-object play. Present no more than three large options,
read every prompt aloud, avoid timers and use warm retry language. A child may stop or hand the device
to an adult at any time.
